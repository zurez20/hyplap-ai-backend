## Is AWS still the best cloud pick for Indian SaaS founders in 2026? ## Quick Read AI-generated summary


If you are building a SaaS product in India right now, you have probably asked yourself this exact question over a cup of chai. The Indian SaaS ecosystem has matured rapidly in recent years, with product-led growth and enterprise solutions scaling faster than ever. While AWS built its reputation as the undisputed king of the global cloud market, the reality for Indian founders in 2026 is far more nuanced. The demands of latency, data residency laws, and local competition have forced a re-evaluation of what actually constitutes the best infrastructure for your startup. We have worked closely with numerous startups navigating this exact transition, and the patterns are clear. A 2025 NASSCOM report highlighted that over 60 percent of Indian SaaS companies now run on at least two cloud environments simultaneously. This shift signals that the era of relying on a single provider is over. For founders in Mumbai, Bengaluru, or Hyderabad, choosing the right cloud stack is no longer just a technical decision but a core business strategy that dictates unit economics and market speed. ## The AWS legacy and local reality


AWS undeniably offers an unparalleled feature set and a massive global footprint that is hard to beat. Its suite of managed databases, serverless compute options, and machine learning tools has enabled countless Indian startups to scale beyond the capabilities of traditional hosting. However, the infrastructure that powers the internet in Europe or North America does not always translate perfectly to the realities of Tier 2 and Tier 3 Indian cities. High latency during peak hours, unpredictable egress fees, and a complex pricing model can quietly erode your margins. Local compliance and data localization laws affecting cross-border data transfers

High outbound data costs eating into your monthly burn rate

Complex billing requiring dedicated DevOps resources to optimize

Latency issues for end-users outside major metro data centers

These factors are driving founders to look beyond AWS and explore platforms that are specifically optimized for the subcontinent. The question is no longer whether AWS is a great product, but whether it is the most practical choice for an Indian SaaS business trying to achieve profitability. As we move further into 2026, the conversation has shifted from simply using AWS to actively managing a multi-cloud strategy. Founders who understand how to leverage local providers for edge computing, combined with AWS for core infrastructure, are gaining a significant competitive edge.
