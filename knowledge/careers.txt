Key Responsibilities:

UI/UX Design Execution: Design intuitive, visually appealing, and highly functional user interfaces for both corporate websites and mobile applications (iOS/Android) using Figma. Visual Asset Creation: Develop all necessary graphic elements, including custom icons, detailed illustrations, and unique design components. Graphic Design Production: Utilize the Adobe Creative Suite (Illustrator, Photoshop, InDesign) to produce high-quality visual content for internal and external projects. Interactive Content: Conceptualize and design engaging, interactive graphics and short animations for social media campaigns using tools like Adobe After Effects. Presentation & Documentation: Create professional and clean presentations of design interfaces and user flows for internal review and client communication. Brand Consistency: Ensure all visual output aligns with established brand guidelines and maintains a consistent, high-quality aesthetic. Communication Skills: Excellent written and verbal communication skills in English. Required Skills & Qualifications:

Software Proficiency (Must-Have):

Expertise in Figma for UI/UX prototyping and collaboration. Strong working knowledge of Adobe Illustrator and Adobe Photoshop for graphic creation and image manipulation. Familiarity with Adobe InDesign for document/presentation layout. Basic to intermediate knowledge of Adobe After Effects for motion graphics/social content.
