## Curious About Hyplap? ## What exactly does HYPLAP do? HYPLAP is a full-service digital agency in Mumbai that builds software products, mobile apps, web platforms, and custom tools — while also handling brand identity, digital transformation, and cloud infrastructure. As of 2025, HYPLAP serves clients across 7+ countries from a single, accountable team. If your business has a digital need, HYPLAP covers it end to end. ## Do you work with startups, or only established businesses? HYPLAP works with both startups and enterprise businesses. We have helped first-time founders launch MVPs on lean budgets and supported enterprise teams running complex, multi-country operations. Our engagement model scales to match where you are today and where you are headed next. ## How much does a project with HYPLAP cost? Project pricing at HYPLAP depends on scope, timeline, complexity, and post-launch support requirements. We offer transparent, flexible pricing with no hidden fees. There is no fixed rate card because every project is different — the fastest way to get an accurate number is to book a discovery call and walk us through what you need. ## How long does a typical project take? Project timelines at HYPLAP vary by type. A branding project typically takes 2–4 weeks. An MVP mobile app runs 8–16 weeks. A full-scale enterprise platform takes 4–9 months. Every project gets a clear timeline before work begins, and we hold to it. ## Can we hire dedicated developers or designers from HYPLAP? Yes. HYPLAP offers dedicated team augmentation — you can bring in a mobile developer, UI/UX designer, cloud engineer, or any specialist you need. They work as a direct extension of your in-house team, with full HYPLAP quality and none of the hiring, onboarding, or retention overhead. ## Do you provide support after the project is launched? Yes. HYPLAP provides ongoing post-launch support covering maintenance, performance monitoring, feature updates, and strategic improvements. We remain actively involved for as long as you need — we do not hand over a deliverable and disappear. ## Is HYPLAP based only in India, or do you work internationally? HYPLAP is headquartered in Mumbai, India, and works with clients across the US, UK, Switzerland, Australia, Kenya, Tanzania, and Uganda. We are built for remote collaboration — time zones are never a blocker, and we have active international client relationships running right now. ## What makes HYPLAP different from other digital agencies? Most digital agencies specialise in one area — only design, only development, or only cloud. HYPLAP delivers all of it under one roof with one team and one point of contact.
