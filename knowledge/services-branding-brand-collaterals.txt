## Frequently Ask Questions



## What makes a brand collateral different from ordinary marketing material? Brand collateral is designed to communicate a business while staying connected to its established brand identity. Ordinary marketing material may focus primarily on the message or campaign, whereas branded collateral also needs to maintain the visual language and consistency of the brand. HYPLAP's Branding approach focuses on creating identities that hold up across screens, print, social, and other communication touchpoints. ## How do Brand Collaterals extend an existing brand identity? Brand Collaterals extend an existing identity by applying its visual direction to the materials a business uses to communicate. The goal is to make each relevant touchpoint feel like part of the same brand rather than an isolated design. HYPLAP positions Brand Collaterals alongside Brand Story & Identity, Brand Stationery, Brand Creatives & Visuals, and Print & Advertising within its Branding services. ## What is the difference between Brand Collaterals and Brand Stationery? Brand Stationery focuses specifically on branded business stationery, while Brand Collaterals address a broader set of branded communication materials. HYPLAP treats them as separate services within its Branding pillar, allowing the scope to be based on what the business actually needs rather than combining every branding requirement into one service. ## How are Brand Collaterals different from Brand Creatives & Visuals? Brand Collaterals focus on extending the brand into communication materials, while Brand Creatives & Visuals focus more broadly on creating visual content for the brand. HYPLAP lists both as distinct services within its Branding pillar, alongside Brand Story & Identity, Brand Stationery, Print & Advertising, and Social Media Management. ## Can you design Brand Collaterals if we already have our logo and brand identity? Yes. An existing brand identity can serve as the foundation for developing consistent branded collateral. The purpose is to extend what already exists rather than automatically redesign the entire brand. HYPLAP's Branding offering separates Brand Story & Identity from Brand Collaterals, so businesses can address their collateral requirements as a specific part of their broader branding needs. ## How do you keep different Brand Collaterals consistent with each other? Consistency comes from applying the same established brand direction across the relevant materials. Elements such as the logo, colour direction, typography, and broader visual language should work together so different materials remain recognisably part of the same brand. This aligns with HYPLAP's approach of building brands that hold up consistently across screens, print, social, and everything in between. ## Do startups need Brand Collaterals, or are they mainly for established companies? Startups can benefit from Brand Collaterals when they need to present a consistent and professional brand across their communication materials. HYPLAP works with startups and founders as well as SMEs, growing businesses, enterprises, and corporates, so the branding requirement can be approached according to the business's current stage and needs. ## Can Brand Collaterals be part of a larger Branding project? Yes. Brand Collaterals can be developed alongside the other services within HYPLAP's Branding pillar. The documented service structure includes Brand Story & Identity, Brand Stationery, Brand Collaterals, Brand Creatives & Visuals, Print & Advertising, and Social Media Management, allowing businesses to build a broader brand presence through connected services. ## Can we hire HYPLAP only for Brand Collaterals instead of the complete Branding service? Yes. HYPLAP's Branding offering is structured into individual services, including Brand Collaterals, so businesses can engage for the specific branding work they need. This is useful when the core brand identity is already established but the business needs that identity translated consistently into additional communication materials. ## How much does Brand Collateral Design cost? Brand Collateral Design is priced according to the project's scope, timeline, complexity, and requirements rather than a single fixed rate. HYPLAP's general pricing approach is flexible because every project is different, with the cost determined after understanding what the business needs and establishing the project scope. ## Lets Talk
