## What does HYPLAP's Brand Creatives service include? HYPLAP's Brand Creatives service focuses on creating standout visual content that communicates your brand clearly and supports visual storytelling. The service sits within HYPLAP's Branding offering alongside Brand Story & Identity, Brand Stationery, Brand Collaterals, Print & Advertising, and Social Media Management. ## What types of brand creatives can HYPLAP design? HYPLAP creates brand creatives tailored to your business, audience, and communication goals. These can support visual storytelling across digital brand touchpoints, with the exact creative formats determined by your campaign, platform, and project requirements. HYPLAP's branding services also cover digital creatives, print, advertising, and social media. ## Can HYPLAP create social media creatives for our brand? Yes. Brand Creatives can support your social media presence with visual content that follows your brand identity and communicates consistently across platforms. HYPLAP also offers Social Media Management as part of its wider Branding services, allowing creative production and social communication to work together. ## Can you create animated or motion-based brand creatives? Yes. HYPLAP's Brand Creatives capability includes motion and design tools such as After Effects and LottieFiles, alongside Adobe XD, Photoshop, Illustrator, and Canva. This allows creatives to be developed for both static visual communication and motion-based storytelling where appropriate. ## Can Brand Creatives follow our existing brand identity? Yes. HYPLAP's branding approach is built around creating identities that remain consistent across screens, print, social, and other customer touchpoints. Brand Creatives can therefore be developed around your established logo, colours, typography, tone of voice, and overall visual direction rather than looking like disconnected designs. ## Can you create brand creatives for a new product or campaign launch? Yes. HYPLAP can create visual assets that help communicate a product, campaign, or business message clearly to its intended audience. The creatives can be aligned with your existing brand identity and adapted to the specific communication requirements of the launch or campaign. ## Can HYPLAP redesign our existing brand creatives? Yes. Existing creatives can be refreshed to better align with your current brand identity, visual direction, and communication goals. The objective is not simply to change the appearance, but to create stronger and more consistent visual communication across your brand touchpoints. ## Can Brand Creatives be used for both digital and print marketing? Yes. HYPLAP's Branding approach is designed to make brands work across screens, print, social, and everything in between. Brand Creatives can therefore support digital communication as well as wider print and advertising requirements, depending on the project scope. ## Can we hire HYPLAP only for Brand Creatives? Yes. Brand Creatives & Visuals is listed as an individual service within HYPLAP's Branding pillar. You can focus on the creative requirements your business needs while also having the option to combine them with Brand Story & Identity, Brand Stationery, Brand Collaterals, Print & Advertising, or Social Media Management. ## How long does a Brand Creatives project take? The timeline depends on the number and type of creatives required. HYPLAP's website states that a typical branding project takes around 2–4 weeks, while individual creative requirements can vary based on scope and complexity. A clear timeline is established before the project begins. ## How much does Brand Creatives design cost? The cost depends on the type, quantity, complexity, and scope of the creative work required. HYPLAP follows a project-based pricing approach rather than a fixed rate card, with the final cost determined according to the specific requirements of the engagement. ## Lets Talk
