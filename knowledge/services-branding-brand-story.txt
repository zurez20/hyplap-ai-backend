## Frequently Ask Questions



## Why should a brand story come before designing the visual identity? A brand story gives the visual identity something meaningful to communicate. HYPLAP's branding approach starts with the strategic foundation — including name, positioning, tone of voice, and brand story — before moving into visual identity through the logo, colour system, and typography. This creates a connection between what the brand stands for and how customers see it, rather than treating visual design as an isolated exercise. ## What should our brand positioning say about us? Your brand positioning should clearly establish how your business wants to be understood and what makes its identity relevant to the people it is trying to reach. It sits within the strategic foundation of HYPLAP's branding approach, alongside your name, tone of voice, and brand story, before those decisions are translated into the visual identity. ## How does tone of voice affect the way customers perceive a brand? Tone of voice shapes how your brand sounds when it communicates with customers and is part of the identity customers experience beyond visual design. HYPLAP includes tone of voice within the strategic foundation of its branding approach, alongside positioning and brand story, so the way a business communicates can remain connected to the identity it presents visually. ## How do you turn a brand story into a visual identity? HYPLAP connects the strategic foundation of the brand with its visual expression through elements such as the logo, colour system, and typography. The process starts with understanding the brand's positioning, tone of voice, and story, then translates those strategic decisions into a visual identity that reflects who the business is and communicates consistently across its brand presence. ## Do I need Brand Story & Identity if my business already has a logo? A logo alone does not represent the complete brand identity. HYPLAP's branding approach covers a wider foundation that includes positioning, tone of voice, and brand story, followed by the logo, colour system, and typography. If your existing logo does not connect clearly with how your business is positioned or communicated, strengthening the wider brand foundation can be more useful than changing the logo alone. ## Can Brand Story & Identity work for an established business, or is it mainly for startups? Brand Story & Identity can be relevant to both new and established businesses. HYPLAP works with startups, SMEs, and enterprises, and its Branding service is designed around creating identities that are sharp, consistent, and built to connect. An established business can therefore use the service to strengthen the foundation behind an existing brand as its business grows or evolves. ## How is Brand Story & Identity different from Brand Collaterals and Brand Creatives? Brand Story & Identity establishes the strategic and visual foundation of the brand, while Brand Collaterals and Brand Creatives support how that identity is executed across different communication materials. HYPLAP lists these as separate services within its Branding pillar, allowing the strategic foundation and the wider brand execution to be handled as connected but distinct parts of the overall branding journey. ## Can Brand Story & Identity be developed as part of a larger branding project? Yes. HYPLAP's Branding service covers the wider brand journey from strategic foundation through visual identity and ongoing brand execution. Brand Story & Identity can therefore sit alongside Brand Stationery, Brand Collaterals, Brand Creatives & Visuals, Print & Advertising, and Social Media Management when a business needs a broader brand presence. ## How long does a Brand Story & Identity project take? A typical HYPLAP branding project takes around 2–4 weeks. The exact schedule depends on the project's scope, and HYPLAP states that every project receives a clear timeline and delivery milestones before work begins. ## How much does Brand Story & Identity cost? The cost depends on the scope and requirements of the branding engagement rather than a fixed rate. HYPLAP states that larger engagements such as full branding projects are scoped and priced according to specific requirements, with cost transparency established before work begins. ## Lets Talk
