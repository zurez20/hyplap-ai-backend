## Frequently Ask Questions



## How can professional branding help my business stand out and build trust? Professional branding helps create a clear and recognisable identity across the places customers interact with your business. HYPLAP's Branding approach connects your brand story, visual direction, and communication so the brand feels consistent across screens, print, social media, and other customer touchpoints. ## We already have a logo. Do we still need a Brand Story? Yes, because a logo alone does not communicate the complete story behind a business. A strong Brand Story can help define what the business stands for, how it should be perceived, and how it communicates with its audience. HYPLAP can develop the brand story around an existing visual identity rather than requiring the logo to be replaced. ## Can HYPLAP create a Brand Story for a new business? Yes. HYPLAP can help a new business develop a clear Brand Story that communicates its purpose, positioning, personality, and message to the intended audience. This can then provide direction for the wider branding and communication created for the business. ## Can HYPLAP refresh the Brand Story of an existing business? Yes. An established business can refresh or strengthen its Brand Story when its positioning, audience, offerings, or business direction has evolved. HYPLAP can work with the existing brand and determine what needs to be clarified or improved without automatically requiring a complete rebrand. ## What is the difference between Brand Story and Brand Stationery? Brand Story focuses on the narrative, positioning, personality, and message behind the business, while Brand Stationery applies the established brand direction to professional business communication materials. They address different parts of the branding process and can be used together when a business needs both strategic brand clarity and consistent business communication. ## What is the difference between Brand Story and Brand Collaterals? Brand Story establishes the narrative and communication foundation behind the brand, while Brand Collaterals extend the brand into business, marketing, and customer communication materials. Keeping them as separate services allows businesses to engage HYPLAP for the specific branding requirement they currently need. ## Can HYPLAP create brand creatives based on our Brand Story? Yes. Brand Creatives & Visuals can translate the brand's story, message, and visual direction into creative communication for digital, social media, campaigns, print, and other marketing requirements. This helps ensure that visual communication reflects what the brand is trying to communicate rather than being disconnected from its underlying message. ## Can HYPLAP handle both branding and social media communication? Yes. HYPLAP provides Social Media Management alongside its other Branding services. Social media content, publishing, analytics, and brand-focused communication can be aligned with the wider brand direction so the business presents a consistent identity across its social and other customer touchpoints. ## Can I hire HYPLAP for only one branding service instead of the complete Branding package? Yes. HYPLAP's Branding offering is structured into individual services, allowing businesses to engage for a specific requirement such as Brand Story, Brand Stationery, Brand Collaterals, Brand Creatives & Visuals, Print & Advertising, or Social Media Management. These services can also be combined when a broader branding requirement exists. ## How much do branding services cost and how long does a branding project take? Branding cost and timeline depend on the scope, complexity, number of services, and specific requirements of the engagement. HYPLAP states that a typical branding project takes around 2–4 weeks, while individual services can vary according to their scope. Project pricing and delivery milestones are established according to the requirements before work begins. ## Lets Talk
