## What is a cloud infrastructure audit and why is it necessary? A cloud audit assesses your current setup for cost inefficiencies, security gaps, performance issues, and scalability limits. HYPLAP conducts full-stack audits across compute, storage, and networking layers to ensure your cloud is optimized, secure, and scalable. Schedule your cloud health check—identify risks before they become problems →


## Do you perform audits for AWS, Google Cloud, and Azure environments? Yes. HYPLAP offers multi-cloud audits across AWS, Google Cloud Platform (GCP), and Microsoft Azure. ## Can HYPLAP help reduce my monthly cloud billing? Absolutely. Most businesses overspend on underutilized resources. We conduct cost optimization audits to downsize instances, eliminate zombie resources, apply reserved pricing models, and recommend auto-scaling. ## What is a cloud readiness assessment and who needs it? A cloud readiness assessment is ideal if you're planning to migrate from on-premise servers or traditional hosting. HYPLAP evaluates your workloads, data volume, compliance needs, and team readiness to recommend the right migration roadmap. ## What kind of security checks are included in HYPLAP’s cloud audits? We review IAM policies, firewall rules, SSL certs, data encryption, backups, and DDoS protections. We ensure your cloud environment meets industry security benchmarks (like ISO, GDPR, SOC 2, etc.). Get your cloud security posture audited and hardened with HYPLAP →


## How frequently should I perform a cloud audit? We recommend a quarterly audit for dynamic infrastructures, and at least bi-annual audits for stable environments. Regular cloud audits ensure you stay aligned with new usage, billing, and compliance trends. ## Can HYPLAP assist with re-architecting my cloud environment post-audit? Yes. After identifying issues, HYPLAP creates a customized re-architecture plan to align your infrastructure with best practices—improving scalability, automation, and cost-efficiency. ## How long does a typical cloud audit or consultation process take?
