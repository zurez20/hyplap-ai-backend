These services cover cloud setup, migration, hosting, infrastructure management, security, backup, optimisation, and ongoing support. ## Why should my business move to the cloud? Cloud infrastructure can help businesses improve scalability, flexibility, performance, security, and operational efficiency without depending entirely on traditional physical infrastructure. HYPLAP helps businesses plan, migrate, manage, and optimise cloud environments according to their workloads and business requirements. ## Does HYPLAP provide both AWS and Google Cloud solutions? Yes. HYPLAP provides both AWS and Google Cloud solutions, including cloud setup, migration, deployment, infrastructure management, optimisation, and ongoing support. The appropriate platform depends on your applications, workloads, technical requirements, and business objectives. ## Can HYPLAP migrate our existing website, application, or server to the cloud? Yes. HYPLAP can help businesses migrate existing applications and infrastructure from traditional hosting, on-premise environments, or other cloud platforms. The migration approach is planned around the existing architecture, workloads, data, security requirements, and desired level of downtime. ## Can HYPLAP integrate cloud solutions with our existing systems? Yes. HYPLAP can integrate AWS, Google Cloud, and other cloud environments with your existing technology stack. The integration approach is planned around your current infrastructure, applications, databases, workflows, and business requirements to maintain compatibility and minimise disruption. ## Does HYPLAP provide cloud security and backup solutions? Yes. HYPLAP provides security and backup solutions covering areas such as cloud security, encryption, automated backups, malware protection, disaster recovery, and infrastructure protection. The exact security and backup configuration depends on your environment, workloads, and business continuity requirements. ## Can HYPLAP audit and optimise our existing cloud infrastructure? Yes. HYPLAP provides Cloud Consultation & Audits to assess existing cloud environments, including architecture, performance, cost, security, compliance, and resource usage.
