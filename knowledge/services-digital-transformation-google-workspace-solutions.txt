## Frequently Ask Questions



## What is Google Workspace, and does my business actually need it? Google Workspace is Google's suite of professional business tools — custom email on your own domain (yourname@yourcompany.com), Gmail, Drive, Docs, Sheets, Calendar, and Meet, all working together under one admin console. If your team is currently using a personal Gmail address for business, sharing files over WhatsApp, or juggling calendars manually, Google Workspace is the fastest way to look and operate like a serious, professional business. ## How much does Google Workspace setup cost? Google Workspace itself is priced per user by Google, starting from a Business Starter plan and scaling up based on storage and features needed. HYPLAP's setup service — domain configuration, mailbox creation, security settings, and data migration — is offered at a fixed price since it's a defined, repeatable scope. As a Google partner, we help you pick the right plan for your team size and budget, so you're not overpaying for features you won't use. ## How long does it take to set up Google Workspace for our business? A standard Google Workspace setup for a new business, including domain verification, mailbox creation, and basic security configuration, typically takes 1–3 business days. If you're migrating existing email and data from another provider, the timeline extends based on the volume of mailboxes and data being moved — HYPLAP gives you a specific timeline once we know your current setup. ## Can you migrate our existing emails and files from our old provider? Yes. HYPLAP handles full migration from providers like Outlook, Zoho Mail, or any existing hosting setup — moving emails, contacts, calendars, and files into Google Workspace with minimal disruption to your team's day-to-day work. We plan the migration to avoid downtime and make sure nothing gets lost in the switch. ## Is Google Workspace secure enough for business data? Yes. Google Workspace includes enterprise-grade security by default — two-factor authentication, spam and phishing protection, and encrypted data storage. HYPLAP configures these settings correctly from day one, including admin controls, access permissions, and backup policies, so your business data is protected without your team having to manage the technical side. ## Do you also handle ongoing management, or just the initial setup? Both. HYPLAP offers ongoing Google Workspace administration as part of our Digital Transformation and Managed Services — adding or removing users, managing permissions, troubleshooting issues, and keeping your setup secure and optimised as your team grows. You get a dedicated point of contact instead of relying on Google's generic support. ## Can Google Workspace work alongside our existing website and domain? Yes. Google Workspace is set up using your existing domain, so your professional email matches your website exactly. HYPLAP handles the DNS and domain configuration required to connect the two cleanly, whether your domain is registered with us or elsewhere. ## Is Google Workspace better than using a free Gmail account for business? For any business serious about credibility, yes. A free Gmail address signals an unestablished or informal operation to clients and partners. Google Workspace gives you email on your own domain, greater storage, admin-level control over your team's accounts, and access to collaboration tools your team already knows how to use — with none of the professionalism gap. ## Can you set up Google Workspace for a team spread across multiple countries? Yes. Google Workspace is built for distributed teams, and HYPLAP regularly sets this up for clients with staff across India, the US, UK, Australia, and East Africa. Admin controls, shared calendars, and Meet all work seamlessly across time zones and locations from a single console. ## What happens if we need help after the setup is complete? HYPLAP doesn't set up your Workspace and disappears. We offer ongoing support for admin issues, new user onboarding, security reviews, and troubleshooting — either as part of a broader Digital Transformation engagement or as standalone support. You always have someone to call instead of navigating Google's support system alone. ## Lets Talk
