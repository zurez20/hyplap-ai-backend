## Frequently Ask Questions



## What does an SEO company actually do? An SEO company improves how well your website ranks in search results, so the people already searching for what you offer can actually find you. At HYPLAP, this covers technical SEO (site speed, structure, indexability), on-page optimisation (content, keywords, meta tags), and off-page work (backlinks, authority building) — all working together, not treated as separate, disconnected fixes. ## How much do SEO services cost in India? SEO services in India are typically priced as a monthly retainer, ranging from ₹15,000–₹40,000 per month for a small business or local SEO programme, to ₹50,000–₹1.5 lakhs or more per month for competitive, multi-location, or e-commerce SEO. Pricing depends on your industry's competitiveness, current site condition, and how aggressive your growth targets are. HYPLAP scopes this after an initial audit, with full transparency on what you're paying for. ## How long does SEO take to show results? SEO is not instant — expect to see early movement (improved indexing, initial keyword ranking gains) within 6–8 weeks, with meaningful traffic and lead growth typically building over 4–6 months. Highly competitive keywords and industries take longer. Anyone promising first-page rankings in a few weeks is either overselling or targeting keywords nobody searches for. HYPLAP sets realistic timelines based on your specific market from day one. ## What's the difference between SEO and just running Google Ads? Google Ads gets you visibility immediately but stops the moment you stop paying. SEO builds organic ranking that keeps generating traffic and leads long after the initial work is done, at a much lower cost per lead over time. The two aren't competitors — many of our clients run both together, using ads for immediate visibility while SEO builds sustainable, long-term ranking underneath it. ## Do you build the website AND handle SEO, or just SEO for existing sites? Both. If HYPLAP builds your website, we implement the technical SEO foundation — proper heading structure, schema markup, site speed, mobile-first design — from the first line of code. If you already have a website built elsewhere, we run a full technical audit, fix what's holding you back, and take over ongoing optimisation from there. ## What is AEO and GEO, and does HYPLAP handle that too? AEO (Answer Engine Optimisation) and GEO (Generative Engine Optimisation) are the newer disciplines of getting your content surfaced directly in AI-generated answers — tools like Google's AI Overviews, ChatGPT, and Perplexity — not just in traditional blue-link search results. HYPLAP structures content to directly answer real questions your customers ask, in the clear, self-contained format these AI systems pull from, alongside traditional SEO — so you're positioned for how people are actually searching today, not just how they searched five years ago. ## Do you guarantee first-page rankings? No, and any agency that guarantees a specific ranking position is not being straight with you — Google's algorithm isn't something any agency controls. What HYPLAP does guarantee is a transparent process: a clear audit, a defined strategy, monthly reporting on rankings and traffic, and consistent execution against a plan built specifically for your market and competition. ## Will SEO work for a business that only serves a local area? Yes — local SEO is one of the highest-ROI forms of search optimization for service-based and location-specific businesses. HYPLAP sets up and optimises Google Business Profile listings, local keyword targeting, and location-specific content, so you show up when nearby customers search for exactly what you offer — whether that's one location in Mumbai or multiple across India. ## What reporting do we get, and how do you measure success? HYPLAP provides monthly reports covering keyword ranking movement, organic traffic growth, indexed pages, and — most importantly — actual leads or conversions generated from organic search, not just vanity traffic numbers. We track against the business outcomes that matter to you, not just ranking positions for their own sake. ## Can you fix a website that's been penalised or dropped in rankings? Yes. HYPLAP diagnoses ranking drops and penalties through a full technical and backlink audit — identifying whether the cause is a Google algorithm update, technical issues, thin or duplicate content, or a manual penalty. Once identified, we build a specific recovery plan and are transparent that recovery timelines vary depending on the cause and severity. ## Lets Talk
