## What is SEO and why is it important for my business website? SEO (Search Engine Optimization) is the process of improving your website's visibility on Google and other search engines. It helps your business rank higher for relevant keywords, drive organic traffic, and get discovered by potential customers—without paid ads. ## Is SEO a one-time activity or an ongoing process? SEO is not a one-time fix—it's a continuous, evolving strategy. Google updates its algorithms regularly, and SEO efforts must be sustained to maintain and improve rankings. Content updates, technical fixes, backlink building, and keyword strategy are ongoing efforts. Invest in long-term SEO growth—HYPLAP is here for the full journey →


## How does Google crawl and rank websites? Google uses bots called crawlers to scan your website pages. It looks at content quality, page speed, meta tags, keywords, backlinks, mobile-friendliness, and user experience. A properly optimized site helps Google index it faster and rank it higher for search queries. ## What is the difference between On-Page SEO, Off-Page SEO & Technical SEO? On-Page SEO: Keywords, content, meta tags, headings, images. Off-Page SEO: Backlinks, social signals, brand mentions. Technical SEO: Site speed, mobile responsiveness, schema, XML sitemap, crawl errors. We provide full-spectrum SEO covering all 3 dimensions—no shortcuts →


## What are keywords and how do they impact search rankings? Keywords are search terms people type into Google (e.g., "digital marketing company in India"). SEO helps your website rank higher for the right keywords so your audience finds you. HYPLAP performs deep keyword research aligned with search intent and business goals. ## What is Local SEO and how can it help Indian businesses? Local SEO targets users in your specific region or city (e.g., "best software company in Mumbai"). It includes Google Business Profile optimization, location-based keywords, reviews, and local citations. It's essential for startups, clinics, retailers, and service-based businesses. ## How do tools like Google Analytics & Google Search Console support SEO? Google Analytics tracks traffic, bounce rates, and user behavior. Google Search Console helps monitor keyword rankings, indexing status, site errors, and mobile usability. HYPLAP integrates both tools into your SEO dashboard for actionable insights. ## What are some SEO terms or "tricks" IT companies use that clients should beware of? Many agencies misuse jargon like "DA boosting," "black-hat backlinks," "spam link exchange," or promise "guaranteed first-page rankings in 10 days." These practices can get your website penalized or deindexed. HYPLAP uses only white-hat, ethical SEO that builds lasting results.
