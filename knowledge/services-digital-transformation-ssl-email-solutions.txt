## What is an SSL certificate and why is it essential? An SSL certificate encrypts data between your website and visitors, making it secure. Google favors SSL-enabled sites in rankings. ## What's the difference between DV, OV, and EV SSL certificates? DV SSL (Domain Validation): Fastest & cheapest, basic encryption. OV SSL (Organization Validation): Includes company verification. ## What is a Wildcard SSL and when should I use it? A Wildcard SSL secures your main domain and all its subdomains (e.g., blog.yourdomain.com, shop.yourdomain.com) under one certificate—ideal for SaaS, portals, or multi-service platforms. Secure every subdomain affordably with Wildcard SSL from HYPLAP →
