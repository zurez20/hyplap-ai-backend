## Frequently Ask Questions



## How much does it cost to build a mobile app in India? Mobile app development costs in India range from ₹3–5 lakhs for a basic MVP with core features, to ₹15–40 lakhs or more for a full-featured enterprise application. The final cost depends on the platform (Android, iOS, or both), feature complexity, third-party integrations such as payments and maps, and post-launch support requirements. At HYPLAP, every project is scoped individually with a transparent cost breakdown provided before any work begins — no hidden charges, no vague estimates. ## How long does it take to develop a mobile app? A focused MVP with core features typically takes 8–12 weeks. A mid-complexity app with multiple user roles and integrations usually runs 12–20 weeks. A full-scale enterprise mobile application can take 5–9 months. Timeline depends on scope, design complexity, and decision turnaround speed during the process. HYPLAP provides a milestone-based project timeline before development begins so every stakeholder knows exactly what to expect and when. ## What's the difference between a native app and a cross-platform app? A native app is built specifically for one platform — Android using Kotlin, or iOS using Swift — offering maximum performance and deep device integration. A cross-platform app is built once using a framework like Flutter or React Native and runs on both Android and iOS, making it faster and more cost-efficient to develop. For the vast majority of business use cases, the performance difference between native and cross-platform is negligible. HYPLAP will recommend the right approach based on your users, budget, and long-term product roadmap. ## Should I build an Android app, iOS app, or both? The right choice depends entirely on your target market. Android holds significantly higher market share in India, making it the logical starting point for India-first products. If you are targeting urban Indian professionals or users in the US, UK, or Australia, iOS carries equal importance. HYPLAP typically recommends launching on one platform first to validate the concept, then expanding once you have real user traction. We give you a specific recommendation based on your market and goals — not a generic answer. ## Do you build MVPs for early-stage startups? Yes, and it is one of our core strengths. A well-built MVP is not a stripped-down product — it is a focused, properly designed application that tests your core hypothesis with real users as efficiently as possible. HYPLAP has taken dozens of founders from idea to first working product in 8–12 weeks. We scope only what is truly essential, build it to production quality, and help you get it into users' hands fast enough to generate meaningful feedback. ## What technologies does HYPLAP use for mobile app development? HYPLAP works across the full modern mobile stack. For native Android we use Kotlin and the Android SDK. For native iOS we use Swift. For cross-platform development we work with Flutter, React Native, and Ionic. Backend services are built on Node.js, Laravel, and Python. Cloud infrastructure and real-time features are powered by AWS, Google Cloud, and Firebase. The technology choice is always driven by what is right for your specific product — not by what is most convenient for us. ## Will my app work offline? Many apps can be built with offline functionality where core features operate without an internet connection and sync automatically when connectivity returns. Whether offline support is worth building depends entirely on your use case and user behaviour. HYPLAP assesses this during the discovery phase and only recommends offline architecture where it genuinely improves the user experience — not as a default addition that inflates scope and cost. ## What happens after my app launches? Post-launch support is included in every HYPLAP mobile app project. After launch, we monitor app performance, resolve bugs, manage app store updates, and help plan the next phase of features. As of 2025, the most successful apps in any category release meaningful updates every 6–8 weeks. HYPLAP stays actively involved to ensure your app improves with every release, not just at launch. ## Can you improve or rebuild an app that already exists? Yes. HYPLAP regularly takes over existing mobile products for performance overhauls, design refreshes, new feature development, platform migrations, and complete rebuilds. Every engagement starts with a thorough technical and UX audit so we understand exactly what we are working with before recommending a path forward. If your app has a problem worth solving, we will find it and fix it. ## How do I get started with HYPLAP? Book a free 30-minute discovery call.
