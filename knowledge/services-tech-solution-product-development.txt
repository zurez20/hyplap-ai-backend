## Frequently Ask Questions



## What does a product development company actually do? A product development company takes your idea from concept to a live, working product — covering market validation, UX design, engineering, testing, and launch, under one team. HYPLAP handles the full journey: we don't just write code against a spec, we help you figure out what to build, why, and for whom, before a single feature is designed. This is different from hiring a freelance developer or a dev shop that only executes — we're involved in the thinking, not just the typing. ## How much does product development cost in India? Product development costs in India typically range from ₹3–8 lakhs for a lean MVP with core features, to ₹15–50 lakhs or more for a full-scale product with multiple modules, integrations, and enterprise-grade infrastructure. The final number depends on platform (web, mobile, or both), feature complexity, and post-launch support needs. HYPLAP scopes every project individually and gives you an itemised cost breakdown before any work starts — no vague estimates, no hidden charges. ## How long does it take to go from idea to a launched product? A focused MVP typically takes 8–14 weeks from kickoff to launch. A mid-complexity product with multiple user roles and integrations usually runs 14–22 weeks. A full-scale, enterprise-grade platform can take 6–10 months. HYPLAP maps out a clear, milestone-based timeline at the start of every engagement, so you always know what's shipping and when. ## Do you help validate the idea, or do you just build what I tell you? We do both — but validation comes first. Before we design or build anything, we work with you to pressure-test the idea: who's the user, what problem are you actually solving, and is there a faster or smarter way to prove it works before investing in a full build. If your idea needs sharpening, we'll tell you — not just take the brief and start coding. ## Can you build an MVP first and scale it later? Yes — this is how most of our product engagements start. We build a focused, production-quality MVP that tests your core hypothesis with real users, then scale the architecture and feature set based on what you learn post-launch. This approach gets you to market faster, costs less upfront, and means every feature you add later is backed by real user data, not guesswork. ## What team do we actually get — is it one developer or a full team? For product development, HYPLAP assigns a cross-functional team — product strategist, UI/UX designer, engineers, and QA — working together under one dedicated project lead. You're not managing five freelancers or a single generalist stretched across your whole build. It's structured like an in-house product team, without the hiring and overhead. ## What technologies do you use to build products? HYPLAP builds across the full modern stack depending on what your product needs — React, Next.js, Angular, and Vue.js on the frontend; Node.js, Laravel, and Python on the backend; Flutter, React Native, or native Android/iOS for mobile; and AWS or Google Cloud for infrastructure. We choose the stack based on your product's requirements and long-term scalability — not based on what's convenient for us. ## Do you only build the product, or do you help with strategy and roadmap too? Both. Product development at HYPLAP includes strategic input — feature prioritisation, roadmap sequencing, and go-to-market readiness — alongside the actual build. If you already have a clear roadmap, we'll execute against it. If you don't, we'll help you build one before development starts, so you're not building features in the wrong order. ## What happens if my requirements change mid-build? They usually do, and our process is built to handle that. We work in short agile sprints with working demos at every milestone, so you see real progress — not just status reports. When scope shifts, we assess the impact on timeline and cost transparently and adjust the plan together. You're never locked into a spec that no longer reflects where your product actually needs to go. ## Do you support the product after it launches? Yes, always. Post-launch support is built into every product development engagement — bug fixes, performance monitoring, user feedback loops, and iterative feature development. A launched product is the start of the relationship, not the end of the project. We stay on as a long-term engineering partner, not a vendor who disappears after go-live. ## Lets Talk
