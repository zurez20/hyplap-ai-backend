## Frequently Ask Questions



## What does a UI/UX design company actually do? A UI/UX design company designs how your product looks and how it works — the visual interface (UI) and the end-to-end experience a user has using it (UX). At HYPLAP, this covers user research, wireframing, prototyping, visual design, and usability testing for clients across India, the US, UK, Switzerland, and Australia, all before a single line of code is written. ## How much does UI/UX design cost? UI/UX design costs typically range from ₹40,000–₹1.5 lakhs for a focused app or website design project, to ₹3–8 lakhs for a full product design system covering multiple user flows, states, and platforms. The cost depends on the number of screens, complexity of user flows, and whether a design system is required. HYPLAP scopes this individually and shares a transparent quote before work begins. ## How long does a UI/UX design project take? A focused design project for an app or website typically takes 2–4 weeks from research to final, developer-ready files. A full product design system with multiple flows and a component library usually runs 4–8 weeks. HYPLAP defines the timeline upfront based on scope, so design doesn't become the bottleneck that delays your build. ## What's the difference between UI design and UX design? UX design is the structure — how a user moves through your product, what they do at each step, and whether the flow makes sense. UI design is the surface — the colours, typography, spacing, and visual details that make that structure look and feel right. HYPLAP treats them as one connected discipline, not two separate handoffs. ## Do you design for both mobile apps and websites? Yes. HYPLAP designs interfaces for Android, iOS, cross-platform apps, websites, web portals, and dashboards — each with its own design conventions and user expectations. Whether you need a single screen redesigned or a full multi-platform design system, we scope it to exactly what you need. ## Will I see the design before development starts? Always. We create wireframes first to lock the structure and flow, then high-fidelity mockups and interactive prototypes so you can see and click through exactly what will be built. You review and approve the design before our engineers write a single line of code. ## Do you conduct user research and testing, or just design based on a brief? Both, depending on what the project needs. For most engagements, we start with lightweight user research before designing anything. For larger or more complex products, we also run usability testing on prototypes with real users, so design decisions are based on how people actually behave, not assumptions. ## What tools do you use for UI/UX design? HYPLAP designs primarily in Figma, which allows for real-time collaboration, interactive prototyping, and a clean handoff to development with exact specs, assets, and design tokens — making remote collaboration with international clients seamless. ## Can you redesign our existing app or website? Yes. HYPLAP regularly takes on redesign projects — auditing your current interface, identifying where users are dropping off or getting confused, and redesigning with a clear rationale for every change, not just a fresh coat of paint. ## Do you hand over design files, or are we locked into working with you? You get full ownership, always. Every wireframe, mockup, prototype, and design file is handed over to you at project completion — including editable source files and a documented design system if one was built. There's no lock-in; you're free to take the files to any development team. ## Lets Talk
