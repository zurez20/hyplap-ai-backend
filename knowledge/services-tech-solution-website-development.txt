## Frequently Ask Questions



## How much does a website cost in India? Website development costs in India depend on what you need. A professional business or corporate website typically ranges from ₹25,000 to ₹1.5 lakhs depending on the number of pages, features, and design complexity. An e-commerce website starts from ₹50,000 and can reach ₹3–5 lakhs for a full-featured store with inventory management and payment integration. Custom web portals and platforms are scoped individually based on functionality. At HYPLAP, every project gets a transparent, itemised quote before any work begins — no surprises, no hidden costs. ## How long does it take to build a website? A professional corporate or business website typically takes 3–5 weeks from brief to launch. An e-commerce website usually takes 4–8 weeks depending on catalogue size and integrations. A custom web portal or platform can take 8–20 weeks depending on complexity. HYPLAP provides a clear milestone-based timeline before work starts and delivers to it — every time. ## Should I use WordPress or a custom-built website? WordPress is the right choice for content-driven websites — blogs, news portals, and brochure sites — where your team needs to update content regularly without developer help. It is cost-effective, widely supported, and sufficient for most standard business websites. Custom development is the better choice when you need specific functionality, complex integrations, high performance requirements, or a user experience that no WordPress template can deliver. HYPLAP will give you an honest recommendation based on your goals — including when the right answer is WordPress and not a custom build. ## Will my website rank on Google? HYPLAP builds every website with full technical SEO foundations in place — correct heading structure (H1, H2, H3), optimised meta titles and descriptions, schema markup, fast load speed, mobile-first design, clean URL structure, and XML sitemap. This gives your site everything it needs to be indexed and ranked by Google. For ongoing SEO — keyword targeting, content strategy, and link building — HYPLAP offers dedicated SEO services separately. A well-built website is the essential starting point, but ranking is an ongoing process that requires consistent effort beyond launch. ## Will my website work on mobile phones? Every website HYPLAP builds is fully responsive — designed and tested to perform seamlessly across all screen sizes, from the smallest smartphone to a wide desktop monitor. We also optimise specifically for mobile performance because Google uses mobile-first indexing, meaning your mobile site is the primary version Google evaluates when determining how to rank your pages. In 2025, a website that underperforms on mobile loses ranking before it ever has a chance to compete. ## Can you redesign my existing website? Yes. HYPLAP redesigns websites regularly — whether you need a visual refresh, a performance overhaul, improved SEO structure, new functionality, or a complete rebuild on a new tech stack. Every redesign starts with a thorough audit of your current site. We give you an honest assessment of what is worth keeping and what needs to change before recommending any scope of work. ## Will I own the website after you build it? Yes, always. Everything HYPLAP builds for you belongs entirely to you — the code, design files, content, and domain. We do not lock clients into proprietary systems or require ongoing fees to access your own website. Full ownership, clean handover, no strings attached. ## Do you provide website hosting? Yes. HYPLAP offers managed web hosting through our Cloud Solutions service, including server setup, security configuration, SSL certificate installation, and ongoing server maintenance. We host on AWS, Google Cloud, or other platforms depending on your requirements and traffic expectations. If you already have existing hosting in place, we can work with that too. ## What happens if I want to make changes to the website after launch? HYPLAP builds every website with a content management system that allows your team to make routine updates — text, images, blog posts, and product listings — without needing a developer. For more complex changes, HYPLAP offers flexible post-launch support retainers so you always have a team to call. We do not hand over a finished site and disappear — we stay involved for as long as you need us. ## Do you also offer SEO services after the website is launched? Yes. Website development and SEO are closely related but distinct disciplines. HYPLAP builds every site with the technical SEO foundations already in place. For ongoing SEO — keyword research, content strategy, on-page optimisation, backlink building, and monthly performance reporting — we offer dedicated Search Engine Optimisation services. As of 2025, businesses that combine a technically sound website with an active SEO programme consistently outperform competitors who treat them as separate, unconnected investments.
