HYPLAP provides mobile app development and web application development, including Android, iOS, cross-platform apps, websites, web platforms, portals, dashboards, CRMs, ERPs, and internal business tools. AI Solutions & Automation is part of HYPLAP's Tech Solutions offering. We can help businesses identify opportunities to use AI and automation to improve digital products, workflows, and business processes, with the specific approach determined by the use case and requirements. ## Can HYPLAP develop custom software for our business? Yes. HYPLAP develops custom web applications and business software around specific workflows, users, data, and operational requirements. Yes. HYPLAP can work with you from the idea and product-definition stage.
