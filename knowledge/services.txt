## Frequently Ask Questions



## How do I know which service is right for my business? The right combination of services depends on your current stage, your gaps, and your growth goals. Most businesses need more than one thing — software, branding, cloud, or all three working together. Book a free 30-minute discovery call with HYPLAP, and we will ask the right questions, map your needs, and recommend exactly what makes sense — no upselling, no pressure. ## What technologies does HYPLAP use for development? HYPLAP works across the full modern tech stack including React, Next.js, Angular, Vue.js, Laravel, Node.js, Flutter, Ionic, and Python. We select the right technology for your specific project requirements rather than forcing your product into a framework that suits us. The stack decision is always driven by what is best for your users, your budget, and your long-term scalability. ## What does HYPLAP's branding service actually cover? HYPLAP's branding service covers the complete brand journey — from strategic foundation (name, positioning, tone of voice, and brand story) through full visual identity (logo, colour system, and typography) to ongoing brand execution including social media management, digital creatives, print collateral, and advertising. You can engage HYPLAP for the full scope or only the parts your business needs right now. ## What is digital transformation and does my business actually need it? Digital transformation means replacing slow, manual, or disconnected business processes with smart, integrated digital systems. If your team manages customer enquiries over WhatsApp, tracks operations in Excel, or lacks a professional communication setup — your business needs digital transformation. HYPLAP has delivered digital transformation projects across retail, logistics, professional services, and healthcare, and the operational improvements are typically visible within the first 90 days. ## Can HYPLAP manage our cloud infrastructure on an ongoing basis? Yes. HYPLAP is a certified partner of both Google Cloud and AWS. We do not simply set up your cloud environment and hand it over — we manage it, monitor it, secure it, and continuously optimise it. For most clients, this means having a dedicated cloud operations team without the cost of hiring one in-house. ## Can we hire a dedicated developer or designer from HYPLAP? Yes. HYPLAP offers dedicated resource augmentation for businesses that already have a product team but need one specific expert — a mobile developer, UI/UX designer, backend engineer, or cloud specialist. Your dedicated HYPLAP professional integrates fully into your team, uses your tools, joins your stand-ups, and works to your roadmap. ## Do you offer fixed packages or is everything custom-priced? HYPLAP offers both. Services with a defined, repeatable scope — such as Google Workspace setup or domain configuration — are available at fixed prices. Larger engagements like mobile app development, full branding projects, or enterprise platform builds are scoped and priced based on your specific requirements. In every case, HYPLAP commits to full cost transparency before any work begins. ## How do you keep projects on track and clients informed? HYPLAP runs all projects on agile methodology with defined milestones, shared project dashboards, and a dedicated project lead assigned to every engagement. You receive regular progress updates and always have a single point of contact who keeps communication clear and consistent from kickoff to delivery. ## How long does a typical project take? Timelines depend on project scope. A branding project typically runs 2–4 weeks. An MVP mobile app takes 8–16 weeks. A full-scale enterprise platform can take 4–9 months. HYPLAP defines a clear timeline at the start of every project and builds delivery milestones into the plan so progress is always visible and measurable. ## Do you provide support and maintenance after launch? Yes, always. Post-launch support is built into every HYPLAP engagement. This covers bug fixes, performance monitoring, feature updates, and ongoing strategic improvements. Launching your product is the beginning of the relationship, not the end — HYPLAP stays involved for as long as you need us. ## Lets Talk
