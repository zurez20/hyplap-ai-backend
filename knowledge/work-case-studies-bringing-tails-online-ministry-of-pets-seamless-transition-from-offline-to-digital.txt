## Bringing Tails Online: Ministry Of Pet’s Seamless Transition from Offline to Digital



## Key Takeaways


A custom ERP solution aligned to industry-specific needs beats one-size-fits-all SaaS tools. Real-time systems reduce friction—enabling scalability without growing pains. Partnering with a niche player like Hyplap ensures faster implementation and better support for pet-care businesses. Combining e-commerce with backend operations can lead to 4x efficiency in order fulfillment. ## The Challenge


Ministry Of Pet, a growing Mumbai-based franchise, was managing operations through spreadsheets, manual entries, and in-store visits. With plans to expand across cities, they needed a centralized inventory management system and a reliable online pet store website builder. Their grooming bookings, sales data, and stock updates were siloed—leading to missed orders, overbookings, and inconsistent customer experiences. ## The Solution


Hyplap stepped in as a trusted pet care app development company, designing a tailored ERP system integrated with a Laravel + Angular-based e-commerce site. Features included:

Inventory automation for real-time stock updates

POS system integration for in-store and online sales

Grooming service management software for appointment tracking

SMS & Email plugins for customer communication and receipts

Responsive UI built with Bootstrap & Typescript for mobile shoppers

This allowed all departments—sales, grooming, purchases—to work in sync, whether online or in-store. ## The Results


The results were immediate and impressive: 60% increase in monthly orders within the first quarter 3x faster grooming appointment booking with real-time availability Centralized control across 4+ retail outlets and warehouses Over 5,000 products managed without manual inventory headaches Seamless customer experience across mobile and desktop Their system is now robust enough to support franchises across multiple cities with zero operational bottlenecks. ## Let’s Bring Your Vision to Life!
