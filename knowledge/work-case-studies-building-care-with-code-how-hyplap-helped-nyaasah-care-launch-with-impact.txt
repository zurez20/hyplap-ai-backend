## The Challenge


Nyaasah Care, a heartfelt startup committed to elderly care services, had no digital foundation when they approached Hyplap. They needed everything—from brand creation to mobile platforms—but more importantly, they needed to look credible and compassionate from day one. Off-the-shelf tools couldn’t capture the sensitivity and trust needed for healthcare startup digital solutions. They required a partner who could think strategically, build holistically, and execute fast. ## The Solution


Hyplap became their digital co-founder—delivering branding, a mobile app, and a dynamic website for healthcare providers all under one roof. Our end-to-end solution included:

Brand design + brand book to define visual tone, logo, and messaging

Dynamic Laravel website to showcase services and build user trust

Mobile app for care services, designed for both families and caregivers

User-friendly UI/UX tailored for elderly users and caretakers alike

Email plugin integration for real-time service inquiries and caregiver assignments

We combined technology with empathy—ensuring the experience was just as human as it was functional. ## The Results


4x increase in qualified service inquiries within the first 2 months

App adoption by 1,200+ users across Android and iOS within the first quarter

Positive brand sentiment from early adopters, backed by a consistent identity

Streamlined caregiver-client matching with in-app service flow

Strong investor interest due to brand and tech coherence

Nyaasah Care is now positioned as a digitally mature, compassionate healthcare startup ready to scale. ## Our Case Study
