## From Failure to Function: How Hyplap Digitally Transformed GTS TLM’s Lubrication Operations



## Key Takeaways


Hyplap successfully took over and delivered a complex field operations mobile app development project for GTS TLM, after a previous attempt by IIT Mumbai failed. We created a dynamic mobile app with an admin panel, integrating AI solutions for precise task scheduling, worker management, and real-time maintenance tracking. The platform, developed by a specialized lubrication management app developer like Hyplap, covers every detail, from equipment onboarding and location tracking across 100+ acre sites to skill-based task assignments and QR code scanning. GTS TLM moved from chaotic manual record-keeping to a streamlined digital system, drastically improving equipment health and operational efficiency, proving Hyplap as a leading maintenance tracking software company. ## The Challenge


GTS TLM faced a monumental challenge: managing lubrication across hundreds of heavy equipment units spread over vast areas. They knew a digital solution was the answer, but their previous efforts had stalled. Failed Previous Attempt: This project had been entrusted to a team from IIT Mumbai, but despite their efforts, the required solution never fully materialized, leaving GTS TLM in a difficult position. Complex Operational Needs: Total Lubrication Management (TLM) is incredibly intricate. It involves numerous lubrication points on heavy equipment, precise cycles, tracking assets across over 100 acres of land, and ensuring the right worker with the right skills performs each task. Chaotic Manual Processes: Before this digital transformation, the entire lubrication process was managed through physical books. This led to lost records, missed lubrication cycles, duplicate work on equipment, and ultimately, preventable equipment deterioration. Ensuring Precision and Accountability: They needed a system that could automate task creation, assign tasks based on worker skills, confirm worker presence, and accurately log every completed activity. ## The Solution


When GTS TLM's project came to Hyplap, it was clear this wasn't just another app development. Shreyas and Rishabh from our team dove deep, understanding the intricacies of their "Total Lubrication Management" process. We knew this was a complex beast, but one we were ready to tame. Taking Over & Delivering: Hyplap accepted the challenge of turning around a previously failed project. Shreyas and Rishabh closely aligned with the GTS team, ensuring every nuance of their workflow was understood from the ground up. Robust Mobile App & Admin Panel: We developed a powerful field operations mobile app development solution complemented by a comprehensive admin panel. This system facilitates equipment onboarding, precise location tracking across vast areas, and detailed management of lubrication cycles throughout the year. AI-Powered Automation: Our solution incorporated AI capabilities for automated task creation, scheduling lubrication frequency, and managing complex lubrication cycles as "tickets." This ensured no task was missed and operations ran smoothly. Smart Worker Management: The system intelligently assigned tasks based on worker skill sets, ensuring only qualified personnel handled specific equipment. It also included checks for worker presence and allowed workers to scan custom QR codes on machines to mark activities as done, providing crucial maintenance tracking. Integrated CMS & LMS (RBAC): The admin panel provided powerful CMS and LMS functionalities. With Role-Based Access Control (RBAC), different users (from clients to field workers) received customized dashboards, ensuring real-time visibility and record-keeping for all. ## The Results


The successful implementation of the Hyplap solution dramatically changed how GTS TLM operates, turning a failed project into a shining success story. Complete Digital Transformation: GTS TLM moved entirely away from inefficient manual bookkeeping. The complex lubrication cycles, once chaotic, are now fully automated and tracked digitally, preventing missed or duplicate tasks. Improved Equipment Health: With precise, managed lubrication, equipment deterioration due to poor maintenance has significantly reduced, extending the lifespan and performance of their heavy machinery. Enhanced Accountability & Efficiency: The mobile app with custom QR codes, skill-based task assignment, and real-time tracking provides unprecedented visibility into field operations, ensuring every lubrication point is covered accurately. Seamless Information Flow: Clients and managers now have real-time access to all lubrication records through various dashboards, providing complete transparency and control over their assets. This project, built with a robust tech stack of Angular, AWS, HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript, Laravel, and Email Plugins, stands as a testament to Hyplap's capability as a leading lubrication management app developer and a truly effective maintenance tracking software company, succeeding where others, even highly regarded institutions, could not. ## Let’s Bring Your Vision to Life!
