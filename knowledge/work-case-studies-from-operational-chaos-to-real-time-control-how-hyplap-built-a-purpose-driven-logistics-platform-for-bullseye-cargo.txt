## From Operational Chaos to Real-Time Control How HYPLAP built a purpose-driven logistics platform for Bullseye Cargo. ## Key Takeaways


Coordination shifted from people to the system — fewer calls, fewer follow-ups, fewer delays. A 4-role mobile app changed field execution — updates happened live instead of over phone calls. Dynamic admin controls replaced manual supervision — workflows, roles, and data stayed structured. Tracking and notifications became automatic — delays, milestones, and exceptions triggered alerts instantly. Dashboards and AI summaries replaced manual reporting — management saw operations as they happened. ## The Challenge


Bullseye Cargo was operationally strong but systemically strained. LR creation happened on calls and Excel sheets. Trip planning required constant coordination between branches, drivers, and vendors. Status updates were buried in WhatsApp chats. Reports were manual and delayed. Each branch functioned in isolation with no integrated solution or intuitive UI/UX to connect daily activities. Management had data but no real-time visibility. The business needed a customized solution with proper system integration — combining mobile app development for field teams with structured websites design and development for centralized control and live tracking. ## The Solution


HYPLAP rebuilt Bullseye Cargo’s operations around a single connected platform. The admin panel became the control center for workflows, roles, and data. The hybrid mobile app empowered drivers, supervisors, vendors, and clients to update trip status in real time. Dashboards gave management live visibility across branches, vehicles, drivers, and trips. Tracking, alerts, reporting, and role-based access were built into the daily flow of work so that coordination happened automatically through the system. ## Technology Stack


Laravel-based admin system

Ionic hybrid mobile app (Android and Ios focused usage)

SMS, Email, and In-app notification engine

AI layer for predictive alerts and generative summaries


## Key Deliverables Solution


Dynamic admin panel with RBAC and workflow automation

4-role mobile app: Client, Vendor, Supervisor, Driver

LR to Trip lifecycle integration for end-to-end tracking

Real-time dashboards for trips, vehicles, drivers, and branches

Central notification system (SMS, Email, In-app)

Automated reporting with AI-generated summaries

Tracking system for delay prediction and smarter allocation

Real-time visibility improved decision-making affecting margins and growth

Reduced manual work lowered operational overhead

Invoices, expenses, and trip-wise payments managed in one place for clear financial visibility and minimal reconciliation effort. ## The Results
