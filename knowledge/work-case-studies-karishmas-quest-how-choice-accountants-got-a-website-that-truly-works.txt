June 04 2025

Karishma and her team faced big problems with their old WordPress website. It was supposed to help them, but it just caused headaches. Old Website Problems: Their previous site was slow, hard to manage, and often had issues. This made them look for a new partner, which is where Hyplap came in. Difficulty with Services: They offer many different services but struggled to show them all online without making the website too heavy or costly to update. They wanted to add new services easily without extra expenses. Poor Online Presence: The old site didn't help them get found by new clients, hurting their brand awareness and stopping them from moving ahead of competitors. They needed a powerful Australian CPA firm digital solution. Hyplap listened to Karishma's problems and came up with a clear, smart plan to give Choice Accountants the online presence they truly needed. Dynamic Website Development: We suggested and built a dynamic website development approach from the ground up. This meant they could manage unlimited services, blogs, and team members through a powerful dynamic admin panel, which acts as their own CMS. It's truly a custom ERP for accountants. SEO & Speed Optimization: The new site was designed to be SEO-optimized from day one, helping them rank higher than their competitors. We also focused on excellent website loading time and speed and ensured a mobile-friendly optimized designed website for all devices. Smart Lead Generation: We set up a clever lead generation strategy with clear questions to make sure the leads they got were organic and truly needed their services. Upgraded Technology: We gave them new, improved technology and server setup along with an SSL certificate for better security and performance. The moment the new website was delivered, Karishma and her team saw a huge positive change. Full Control & Efficiency: They now have a fantastic website with a unique design. The dynamic admin panel lets them see leads, update their status, and easily add new blogs, services, and team members, solving their previous problems. Increased Online Visibility: The SEO-optimized and fast-loading site has significantly boosted their brand awareness and helped them rank higher than competitors. Better Lead Quality: The smart lead generation strategy ensures they get more actual, organic leads, which truly meet their business needs. Future-Ready Platform: Choice Accountants can now grow their services without worrying about website limitations or extra costs, thanks to the scalable dynamic website development approach from Hyplap. We built this powerful solution using tried-and-true tools like HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript, Laravel, and handy Email Plugins. Hyplap is proud to have helped Karishma and Choice Accountants achieve their digital goals, making their online journey smooth and successful. Consult With Us
