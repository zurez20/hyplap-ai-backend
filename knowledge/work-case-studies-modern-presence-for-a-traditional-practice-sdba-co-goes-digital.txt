## The Challenge


SDBA & CO, a respected Chartered Accountancy firm in Mumbai, had no meaningful online footprint. They needed a site that would match the credibility and expertise of a top-tier CA firm—but without complex backend systems or frequent content updates. They weren’t looking for a blog-heavy CMS; they wanted something fast, clean, and aligned with their professional identity. Most importantly, they needed to differentiate themselves visually in a market flooded with templated finance websites. ## The Solution


As experts in professional services web development, Hyplap created a static website for financial firms that emphasized speed, security, and clarity. The site was built using HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript, and Laravel (for routing & form handling). Highlights included:

Animated UI for corporate websites to make key service areas visually engaging

Clean and responsive layout for mobile and desktop

Email plugin integration for quick client inquiries

Crisp, minimal content structure to reduce cognitive load

Easy-to-navigate service presentation with soft interactions

The final product projected authority, trust, and approachability—essential traits for a CA firm. ## The Results


85% improvement in average time spent on site due to better UI/UX

3x more inquiries per month through the embedded contact form

Improved Google presence with basic SEO optimization

Stronger perception of professionalism during client prospecting

Zero site maintenance required—making it ideal for their non-technical team

SDBA & CO now stands out with a digital presence that truly reflects their caliber. ## Our Case Study
