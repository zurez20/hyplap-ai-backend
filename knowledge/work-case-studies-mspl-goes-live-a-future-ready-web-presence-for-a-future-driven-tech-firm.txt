## The Challenge


MSPL, a Mumbai-based cloud and infrastructure solutions provider, had strong technical capabilities but lacked a professional online presence. Their outdated website failed to convey their modern, enterprise-grade services. As a result, potential B2B clients found it hard to trust their digital-first capabilities. MSPL needed a clean, corporate site tailored for IT services companies, with emphasis on clarity, motion, and credibility. ## The Solution


Hyplap delivered a dynamic website for tech firms, powered by Laravel and enhanced with AOS (Animate on Scroll) and Bootstrap 5 to ensure responsive performance and engaging user flow. Key features included:

Service-focused layout with clear value propositions

AOS animations to create subtle motion and guide attention

Contact & lead capture forms integrated with Laravel + email plugin

Modern color palette and iconography to reflect trust and professionalism

Optimized site structure to improve SEO and lead generation for cloud services

The site wasn’t just visually appealing—it was built to convert and scale. ## The Results


2.5x increase in service inquiries within the first 90 days

Improved client confidence and stronger RFP engagement

Bounce rate reduced by 40% due to better UX and navigation

Positioned MSPL as a modern player in cloud & infrastructure services

Website now serves as a key conversion asset for sales and marketing
