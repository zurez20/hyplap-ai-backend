## RNLES Goes Digital: Redefining Education Access in Latur



## Key Takeaways


An intuitive, well-branded website is critical for schools outside metro cities to grow visibility

GSAP animations add life and interactivity—especially effective for young parent audiences

Strategic use of Laravel and Bootstrap keeps sites dynamic yet easy to maintain

Schools benefit from websites that are ERP-ready, ensuring future scalability


## The Challenge


RNLES, a progressive school in Latur, was struggling with outdated communication methods—brochures, word-of-mouth, and occasional Facebook posts. In an increasingly digital-first education landscape, they lacked a reliable and attractive online platform. Parents couldn’t access curriculum details, updates, or facilities information easily, and online inquiries were nearly nonexistent. RNLES needed a complete digital facelift to stay competitive and accessible. ## The Solution


As a trusted school website development company in India, Hyplap stepped in with a dynamic Laravel-powered website, built with modern animations and responsive design. Key features included:

Smooth GSAP animations to create a lively, engaging experience

Program and facility showcase with visual storytelling

Mobile-first responsive design using Bootstrap 5

Integrated email plugin for admission inquiries

Multilingual-ready architecture for wider community reach

Foundation for future integration with Education ERP software for schools

The result? A clean, intuitive, and visually impressive website tailored to education needs. ## The Results


3x increase in monthly admission inquiries after launch

Visitors now spend 4.5x more time on the site due to engaging UI and animations

Parents frequently use the platform to access facilities and program details

Enhanced brand image helped attract more qualified teaching staff

Laid the groundwork for future coaching institute website design modules

RNLES now leads Latur’s school space in digital presence and accessibility. ## Our Case Study
